Frequently asked questions

Speech-to-text isn't working, what should I do?
Check that the app has been granted microphone and speech recognition permission under Settings → Privacy on your iPhone. Also make sure you have an internet connection, since some speech recognition requires it.
Where are my recordings stored?
All recordings are stored locally on your device, not in any cloud we control. To save space on your iPhone, the audio of recordings without a color bookmark is deleted after 7 days (in Text mode, the text stays) — bookmark a recording with a color to keep it permanently.
Can I restore a previous purchase?
Yes. Open the settings menu (the gear icon) in the app and tap "Restore Purchase".
Which languages does speech recognition support?
Dictamen supports dictation in 13 languages: English, Swedish, Finnish, Norwegian, Danish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Japanese. Choose a language with the globe icon on the main screen.
How do I keep recording when I leave the app?
In Voice mode, tap the phone button (Background) while recording, so that it shows "Background on". It is off by default and resets for every recording. Phone calls and alarms pause the recording, and you tap Resume when you are ready. Text mode pauses when you leave the app.
Recording will not start in my car.
In some cars the Bluetooth hands-free takes over the microphone, and the car may try to make a call. Turn Bluetooth off on your iPhone, or disconnect it from the car, and try again.
