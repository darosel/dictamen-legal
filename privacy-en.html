Introduction

Dictamen ("the app", "we") is a voice recording and speech-to-text app. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what choices you have. Dictamen is built to work mainly on your device — we do not operate any server that collects or stores your recordings.

Microphone access

The app needs access to the microphone to record audio. Audio is recorded and stored locally on your device. We have no access to your recordings and do not send them to any servers of our own.

Speech recognition (speech-to-text)

When you use Text mode, your speech is sent to the operating system's built-in speech recognition service (Apple's Speech framework on iOS) to be converted into text. This processing takes place on Apple's own infrastructure and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us. Dictamen only stores the resulting text and/or the audio file locally on your device afterwards.

Local storage

Recordings, transcribed text, bookmarks and settings are stored locally on your device using standard app storage tools (AsyncStorage and the device file system). None of this data is sent to us or to any third party, except as described above for speech recognition.

  • Audio files of untagged recordings are automatically deleted after 7 days to save space.
  • Recordings you bookmark with a color are kept permanently until you delete them yourself.
  • You can delete individual recordings at any time, or delete the app to remove all data immediately.

Purchases

Any in-app purchases (for example, unlocking full access) are handled entirely by the Apple App Store and are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment card details.

Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. The only external processing that takes place is the speech recognition described above, which is performed by the operating system's own services, not by us.

Children

Dictamen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new date at the top of this page.
